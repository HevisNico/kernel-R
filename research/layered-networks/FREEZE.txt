ROUND (the rule; no fixed time)
  ROUND is the first quicknet round whose time, 1692803367 + (ROUND - 1) * 3, is at least 600 s after t_push.
  t_push is the GitHub push time of the FIRST commit on branch evidence/combined-draw that adds DRAW_ANNOUNCE.txt.
  So ROUND = ceil((t_push + 600 - 1692803367) / 3) + 1 (beacon_verify.py round), with t_push in seconds UTC.
  - The coordinator pushes that commit once, alone, after the rehearsal. Only the first such commit counts; the
    branch is append-only.
  - The coordinator draws.
  - It verifies the round with the frozen beacon_verify.py against the pinned key above: the BLS signature, and
    randomness = sha256(signature).
  - It pushes DRAW.json carrying the round, its signature beside its randomness, and the announce commit's push
    time, so that anyone can recheck the draw offline.
  - FALLBACK: if both api.drand.sh and api2.drand.sh are down at draw time, the owner fetches the round from any drand
    relay, and the frozen beacon_verify.py verifies it against the pinned key. The source of the round does not matter.
