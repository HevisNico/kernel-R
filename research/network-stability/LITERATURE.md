# Short literature check

- **Positive linear systems.** Nonnegative matrices preserve the nonnegative orthant in discrete time. Positive systems provide a classical setting for componentwise comparisons and weighted linear Lyapunov functions. The R write guard and the actual production of routed work still need a proof. [Farina and Rinaldi, *Positive Linear Systems: Theory and Applications* (2000)](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781118033029)
- **Small gain and M-matrices.** For a fixed finite nonnegative matrix $G$, $\rho(G)<1$, a strictly positive left subeigenvector with strict margin, and $I-G$ being a nonsingular M-matrix are equivalent. With $D=\operatorname{diag}(\delta_i)$, use $G=AD^{-1}$ to read the column inequality in GOAL. Across sizes the weights and strict margin need uniform quantitative bounds. Nonlinear ISS small-gain results use gain operators to certify stability of interconnections. ISS itself gives no exact write count or terminal read invariance. [Berman and Plemmons, *Nonnegative Matrices in the Mathematical Sciences*, Ch. 6](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611971262.ch6) · [Dashkovskiy, Rüffer and Wirth, *An ISS Small-Gain Theorem for General Networks* (2007)](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0506434)
- **Monotone and abelian dynamics.** Knaster–Tarski gives a least fixed point on a complete lattice for a monotone self-map. It permits other fixed points. The closer precedent for an asynchronous additive work model is least action for abelian networks, which obtains order-independent outcomes from local processing laws. [Tarski, *A Lattice-Theoretical Fixpoint Theorem and Its Applications* (1955)](https://doi.org/10.2140/pjm.1955.5.285) · [Bond and Levine, *Abelian Networks I* (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1137/15M1030984)
- **Tropical / max-plus.** Max-plus models describe certain synchronized event systems. The maximum cycle mean governs the max-plus spectral problem and can help when a timing recurrence uses max of arrival times plus delays. Additive spillover sums the contributions of simultaneous branches. A test of every individual cycle product can miss accumulation. For example the $3\times3$ matrix with zero diagonal and off-diagonal $3/5$ has all cycle products below one and ordinary spectral radius $6/5$. [Baccelli, Cohen, Olsder and Quadrat, *Synchronization and Linearity* (1992)](https://repository.tudelft.nl/record/uuid%3A1add64d4-0dfc-4b5d-9db0-2d4be33dfeb3) · [Butkovič, *Max-algebra: the linear algebra of combinatorics?*](https://web.mat.bham.ac.uk/P.Butkovic/max%20texts/Max%20algebra%20primer.pdf)

The candidate contribution is a Lean bridge from R's pulse, guard and write semantics to a uniform feedback theorem with interior memory bounds, an explicit write budget and exact need under simultaneous bounded read errors. The potential argument and least-action proof for the discrete milestone are classical. This check establishes mathematical precedents and leaves priority for R-specific formalizations open.
