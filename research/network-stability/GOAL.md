# Target theorem — conjecture

For every finite $N$, including directed cycles and self-loops, certify a fixed R training episode from its local cell contracts and a coupling certificate. Fix the initial memories, the training inputs and the routing policy before varying the read errors. The theorem concerns memory during and after training.

**Model.** Vertices are `Fin N`. A directed edge is a causal delivery from a source FIRE to a specified receiving cell and port. The router, delivery delays, pulse inputs and admissible event policy determine which probes occur. Each cell has a uniformly bounded number of stored coordinates. The learning state records log-gains $z_{ip}$, recovery memories $b_i$, counts of completed writes, and the pending training work needed to decide which probes can run next. A native execution also carries the real-valued charge, energy, time, modes and delivery queues. A proposed abstraction relates native execution prefixes to learning steps at cuts where writes are accounted for. It must cover intermediate states and executions that continue to FIRE after learning.

**Local hypotheses.** Initial memories lie in a common interval $[L,U]$, strictly inside their corresponding device rails. Each active probe satisfies the local timing, readiness, charge, energy and headroom bounds required by its cell law. A completed write is one exposed fixed dose on the corresponding stored coordinate, counted once. Probes that produce no write have no unaccounted effect on the write budget. At probe $k$, let $s$ be the clean local read score (peak minus firing level) at that state. The abstract decision is FIRE iff $s+e_{i,p,k}\ge0$, with $-\epsilon^-_{ip}\le e_{i,p,k}\le\epsilon^+_{ip}$. The native-to-abstract bridge must justify that test. It can vary adversarially over cells and probes, including with history. For a clean-score enclosure $[\underline s,\overline s]$ that includes the effect of routed inputs, silence is certified by $\overline s+\epsilon^+_{ip}<0$ and firing by $\underline s-\epsilon^-_{ip}\ge0$. Doses and initialization are held fixed across error traces. Dose errors would require a separate theorem for exact counts and a bound on final analogue memories.

**Coupling hypotheses.** At learning cuts, $q_i\in\mathbb N$ measures remaining chargeable work, including pending work attributable to that cell. Initially $q_i\le Q$. For a write at $i$, let $\delta_i>0$ be its net local retirement, including any self-loop, and $A_{ji}\ge0$ its spillover bound into $j\ne i$, with $A_{ii}=0$. Require $q'_j+\mathbf1_{j=i}\delta_i\le q_j+A_{ji}$. Non-writing actions must preserve or decrease the potential, or be included in a charged macro-step with a proved bound. Require positive weights $1\le w_i\le W$ and a margin $\eta\ge\eta_0>0$ such that $w_i\delta_i-\sum_j w_j A_{ji}\ge\eta$ for every $i$. $A$ measures work caused by a write and needs a proof from the router and cell laws. Uniform headroom and an interior-state invariant must follow from checkable local conditions. A progress lemma must exclude unfinished runs that stall or take infinitely many non-writing steps. A confluence or comparison lemma must establish that permitted interleavings and admissible read errors preserve the same learning outcome.

**Target conclusions.** There exist constants $L,U,C$ fixed independently of $N$ and a terminal write-count vector $m$ computable from the nominal input, local parameters and couplings. For each admissible error trace there exists a maximal legal run from the given initial state. Every such run satisfies the following.

1. **No saturation.** Every stored learning coordinate stays in $[L,U]$ throughout the run, including partial writes, with positive clearance from its device rails.
2. **No divergence of learning.** The run reaches a cut after which no further write is possible under the episode's inputs. The total number of writes is at most $C N$. The proposed potential gives $C=\lceil WQ/\eta_0\rceil$ once every write is charged.
3. **Unique predictable memory and network need.** Every run reaches the same $m$ and the same terminal stored values, determined by the initial memories and the per-coordinate fixed doses. In particular the noiseless run yields that same vector and those same values. The theorem also needs a local decision specification connecting $m$ to the certified read outcomes.

Here **no chaos** means finite-time stabilization of stored memory, with a reproducible terminal result under scheduling and bounded read errors. A deterministic finite-state system eventually becomes periodic, which still allows a nontrivial cycle or different terminal outcomes after a changed read. Native R also has real-valued state. The claim is about the learning state; recurring FIRE events can remain compatible with frozen memory.
