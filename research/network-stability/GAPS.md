# Gaps, highest risk first

1. **Extract a learning step from feedback executions.** There are two-cell reciprocal constructions, a native self-route and arbitrarily many prepared rounds. The arbitrary-size composition results use forward or prescribed chain routing. Establish a write-to-work ledger for arbitrary directed feedback, including what occurs on FIRE without a write, overlapping pulses, delivery multiplicity, refractory periods, self-loops and inactive or clipped ERROR/MARK sections. The columns of $A$ must bound actual work generation after net local retirement. If delivered pulses cannot be covered by the local isolated-probe contracts, make the admission or separation condition explicit and prove it for a nontrivial family of cyclic networks.
2. **Prove terminal agreement under feedback and errors.** Complete native execution uniqueness compares runs with the same start, router, parameters and horizon. It supplies no comparison between error traces. Positive spillover and a decreasing potential alone give no terminal confluence. In particular, a positive additive contribution to FIRE can *disable* an ERROR write, so positivity of the wires does not establish monotonicity of the learning transition. Pick a canonical nominal predictor, then prove an order-independence or comparison lemma at the chosen cuts. Derive a uniform bound for the read perturbation at those cuts and close the strict silence and weak FIRE margins. If the proof relies on keeping an entire routing trace fixed, derive trace preservation by a first-differing-event argument or restrict the protocol.
3. **Obtain strict headroom with uniform constants.** Native capped gain and recovery and the clipped ERROR transfer already give useful bounds. Reaching the cap is compatible with these theorems. A global $O(N)$ budget allows one coordinate to receive $O(N)$ writes. Prove a separate per-coordinate bound on actual writes or a local interior barrier, and extend it across partial sections. Track recovery writes as well as gain writes. A size-dependent weight ratio or margin cannot give the claimed uniform coefficient.
4. **Turn finite-prefix accounting into progress.** The supervisor's `Run` counts corrections in a finite ledger. A physical event budget prevents one kind of Zeno behavior under its hypotheses. Neither ensures a pending training probe completes or that feedback cannot keep creating work in uncharged events. Prove episode existence and progress, including a fair or work-conserving policy and a bound on uncharged work between learning cuts. Specify the distinction between a write-free terminal learning state and later signal traffic.
5. **Transport the result across parameter and observation models.** PR #30 supplies symbolic local flow and counting tools with a common constants record. It has drifted from the baseline. Plan a separate port only if those tools are needed. In either model, fixed-dose counting is an exact-memory bridge only after proving section coverage and accounting for caps. If dose, initial state or timing is allowed to vary, specify the observable that can remain exactly equal before stating a robustness claim.

## Why the checker examples do not settle these gaps

`CompositionCheck.two` has positive off-diagonal damage in both directions, yet its result is acceptance of arithmetic data. `CompositionCheck.chain` has damage 4 to each neighbor and retirement 5. With equal weights the middle column spills 8. For this particular symmetric three-node matrix, even arbitrary positive weights cannot yield strict decrease: the two endpoint inequalities imply $5(w_0+w_2)>8w_1$ and the middle implies $5w_1>4(w_0+w_2)$, which combine to $25w_1>32w_1$. That is an algebraic observation for the phase-0 review. The existing Lean theorem checks the supplied equal-weight witness only. Rejection says nothing on its own about any execution of an R network.
