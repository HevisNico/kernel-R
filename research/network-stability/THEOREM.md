# Stability of guarded learning on routed networks

**Lean theorem.** [NetworkTheorem.lean](NetworkTheorem.lean) states the combined result as `NetworkStability.AutonomousFeedback.Schedule.network_memory`. It covers the discrete fixed-dose model.

Fix $Q,d,B,U,W,\eta$ independently of the number of cells $N$. A probe carries a target cell, a bias, a routed contribution, and a list of successor probes. A scheduler selects a pending occurrence. Its score is its bias plus its routed contribution plus $d$ times the target's write count. An errored score of at least zero produces FIRE and enqueues the successors. A negative score writes one dose and emits no successor. Stored memory is birth memory plus $d$ times the count. All runs share the seed queue and router.

**Hypotheses.** A local reserve assigns $r_i(p)$ to each probe $p$ and cell $i$. A FIRE's successors consume at most the probe's reserve coordinatewise, and a probe has at least one unit for a write at its target. Each cell has at most $Q$ reserve in the seed. Birth memories are at most $B$ and $B+dQ<U$. Every reachable clean configuration satisfies either one pending occurrence per cell, or the interleaving margin for distinct probes targeting the same cell. The latter allows one preceding dose for the local diamond. In general, a silent read of nominal score $s$ remains silent after up to $k$ additional writes if $s+dk+h<0$, where $h$ bounds its positive error. A firing read remains firing if $s+\ell\ge0$, where $\ell$ bounds its negative error.

**Conclusions.** Every reachable memory has at most $Q$ writes per cell and $QN$ overall. It stays between birth memory and $B+dQ<U$. Under the one-sided read margins, a fixed nominal FIFO prefix preserves its full decisions, routing and writes under admissible errors. More generally, a trace whose selected reads carry those margins projects onto a clean schedule. Every certified execution has a finite cut after which its memories are constant. Any two continuation-fair certified executions have the same stable write vector, hence the same limiting memory.

If a scalar rank also assigns $w(p)\ge\eta+\sum_{q\in D(p)}w(q)$ to every probe, with $\eta>0$ and seed weight at most $WN$, FIFO exhausts its queue by $WN$ steps for any error trace, and every reachable clean configuration has a finite continuation to an empty queue. Every exhausted certified schedule has the same stored memory, without a fairness premise. This sharper termination result ranks declared probe types acyclically even when the cell wiring is cyclic.

**Fairness for perpetual traffic.** For each execution prefix and each legal finite continuation from it, each cell's resulting write count must eventually be matched or exceeded by the actual execution. This is an explicit continuation fairness assumption. It does not follow just from the local diamond. After the actual counters freeze, fairness makes the cut write-free under every continuation. The strong diamond joins any two finite prefixes and therefore identifies two such cuts. The [starvation example](FairnessExamples.lean) has valid reserves and local diamonds: both schedules keep processing forever, yet their final counts differ because one never processes the pending training probe.

**Scope.** The proof does not identify chronological logs across different orders or give a universal deadline for the last write under perpetual traffic. The [per-port lift](PortMemory.lean) covers independent port and recovery coordinates. The [native bridge plan](NATIVE_BRIDGE.md) records the remaining refinement obligations.
