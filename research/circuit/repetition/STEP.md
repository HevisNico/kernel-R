**Rule for the step of the campaign.** 400 ns is adopted only if, in every comparison of 400 ns with 200 ns, the FIRE of every read, the counts and the latch and steering counts are the same, every gain moves by at most 0.0002, `eps3` by at most 0.0001 (constants refitted, and frozen), and every latch date by at most 0.001 unit. Result: **200 ns is kept.**

**Integration margin.** Between 200, 100 and 50 ns, `eps3` moves by at most 0.000015 (constants refitted) and 0.000010 (constants frozen at 200 ns), against `MG` = 0.0001: prediction R1 **holds**. This is the sensitivity to the step over three settings, not a bound on the error against the exact solution.
