# Focused research provenance

The physical R definition and the unchanged Decimal interval core originate from
[HevisNico/a22-going-further](https://github.com/HevisNico/a22-going-further), base
commit `269483eb2c6227b369cffb2c46f86eaae5272bb8`. Author and license notices are retained.

The original model definition snapshots, archived kernel guide and exact dependencies of
the new certificates are kept here. Five reusable upstream Lean lemma modules
are also vendored, unchanged, under `proof/lean/`; their original paths and Git
blob identities are recorded in `proof/lean/evidence/UPSTREAM.json` and checked
by `proof/lean/scripts/check_sources.py`. The complete A22 engines, inherited network
proof archive and old global campaign records remain upstream. In particular,
historical executable locations in the byte-identical
[kernel guide snapshot](../kernel/reference/A22_KERNEL_README.txt) and the entries
of `kernel/KERNEL_SHA256SUMS` identify that upstream reference; they do not imply
those engines are present here. The current `kernel/README.md` describes the cell
and the topologies studied in kernel-R. The local-learning source manifest
references the archived guide so its original document hash remains checkable.

This repository adds the reciprocal prototype, teaching-choice and stopping
experiments, their written arguments and local interval certificates. The
READY birth-family/pulses-only entry proof covers the prefix through time 459.9.
The subsequent C->B->A cadence proof composes that authenticated result with C's
birth and the later pulse blocks through 789.9, on 45-parameter physical boxes.
It includes a retained period-4 probe and a written route-cut argument.
The N-cell theorem adds a closed packet contract and a written induction for
every finite directed chain, with an explicit source-length and summed-jitter
budget. Its new scalable preparation schedule reuses the authenticated entry
proof's single-cell arguments; it is not the original fixed birth calendar.
These results do not certify settings-copy ordering, the open reciprocal phase,
adaptive pedagogy, silicon behavior or the full original A22 network at ±10%.

Recorded numerical data are supplied in the pinned English prototype ZIP.
`tools/prepare_recordings.py` validates its identity and complete inner manifest,
then restores required experiment inputs without overwriting different local
work. This avoids duplicating the same evidence across many source-tree files.
Historical source hashes describe the source at each run, not an assertion that
every old pilot can be rerun with a later version of a script.

The interval arithmetic cores and mathematical certificate data remain unchanged.
The entry, cadence and N-cell composition dependency launchers now execute temporary snapshots of
only the authenticated source bytes, with isolated Python startup. Their source
identities, entry anchors, dependent certificates and replay reports have been
regenerated together; bounds, obligations, pulse traces and conclusions were
compared to the preceding commit and are identical. These certificate revalidations do not alter the mathematical claims.

Portability edits concern packaging and optional viewer QA: the source recording
fragment is included, outputs go into `dist/`, and QA uses repository-relative
paths. The local SOURCE_MANIFEST files still match their retained reference files.

The numerical cell simulator now consumes terminal physical events before
returning the final state and checkpoints. New adaptive runs lock both engines
and Python/NumPy/SciPy versions in addition to their controller sources. The
adaptive experiment was then rerun from scratch with this complete manifest,
using the original three training replicates and two 160-pupil lots. All 127
checks and the continuous native-teacher verification pass. The reproduced
scores are 140/160 and 147/160, with 882 and 873 student pulses. This is a
computational reproduction on the original seeds, not new independent evidence.

The current bundle replaces the old adaptive records and excludes legacy
adaptive development sweeps and aborted-run files. They remain in earlier Git
revisions. Other experiments and certificate data retain their separate
provenance. The new adaptive `REPRODUCTION.json` records the execution and
artifact identities; incomplete legacy plans remain refused.

The original full A22 campaign was not freshly rerun to completion during this
session. The new proofs are computer-assisted written arguments with independent
arithmetic replay, not full Lean formalizations or independently peer-reviewed
mathematics. The proposed invention protocol is not a completed experiment.

The [partial Lean formalization](../proof/lean/README.md) adds checked lemmas and
exact endpoint comparisons while retaining those boundaries. Generated Lean
evidence comes directly from the committed certificates and the verified ZIP;
`proof/lean/evidence/GENERATED_FROM.json` records the input SHA-256 identities.
Neither exact endpoint checks nor replay of recorded Boolean outcomes proves
the missing correspondence with complete hybrid trajectories.

The Lean cadence extension consumes both `results/reciprocal_cadence/`
certificates directly, including their 95-case pulse traces. The new combined
MARK/ERROR case is modeled separately from the earlier three-case abstraction.
The updated bundled archive remains checked by `tools/prepare_recordings.py`;
the adaptive evaluation identity now refers to the new execution. The Lean
recorded-outcome propositions are regenerated from those records; their
finite counts do not prove the underlying continuous trajectories.
