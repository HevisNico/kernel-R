# A rigorous next test of lesson invention

Status: proposed protocol, not an implemented or completed experiment.

## Keep the claims separate

1. **Selection:** choose a complete supplied lesson. Demonstrated in folder 07.
2. **Adaptation:** use observations during a lesson to change its stopping time. Demonstrated within the supplied block protocol in folder 08.
3. **Synthesis:** generate pulse times or settings transfers that were not a complete supplied template. Not yet demonstrated.
4. **Useful invention:** synthesized lessons improve held-out learning under matched resources and information. Not yet demonstrated.

A different schedule is not automatically a useful invention. Tiny jitter or a
new ordering can establish numerical novelty without pedagogical benefit.

## Define the controllable space before testing

A possible next space is up to ten canonical pulses with variable arrival times,
first pulse at zero, a fixed deadline, and a minimum separation satisfying every
learner's auxiliary recovery obligation. Pulse shape stays canonical. The teacher
may choose the next delay using already observed pupil responses.

Any optional settings transfer must have a declared port mapping, physical caps,
measurement/copy delay and resource charge. The environment must not inject a
useful parameter setting while calling it a lesson invented by the teacher.

Identify exactly what is learned in R, what is learned by any external optimizer,
and what remains a fixed decoder or sequencer. A search algorithm that constructs
a schedule outside R must be credited as such.

## Two independent success criteria

**Novelty:** after alignment to the first pulse, quantify distance from every
complete training-library schedule under a prespecified representation and
tolerance. Report nearest templates and exact generated schedules. Distinguish a
new continuous timing vector from a new composition of supplied primitives.

**Teaching benefit:** compare retained pupil skill and failure rates on untouched
learners under a common deadline and explicit limits on teaching pulses,
assessment pulses, teacher queries, parameter copies and training trials.
Improvement must survive the strongest applicable baseline, not just a weak
untrained teacher.

## Required comparisons

- A fixed lesson optimized on development pupils with the same training-query budget.
- A conventional feedback controller using the same observable signals.
- The supplied curriculum library and its development-selected best member.
- Learned policy with pupil feedback delayed, reassigned or removed.
- Exact generated lessons replayed to other pupils with matched pulse-count and timing distributions.
- Frozen teacher plasticity, frozen pupil plasticity and transferred-policy controls.
- An oracle reference, clearly separated from deployable comparisons.

If an external optimizer is used, compare its performance with and without R as
the memory or learning substrate under matched information and compute budgets.
That comparison addresses the specific interest of the physical kernel.

## Freeze the evaluation

Choose observations, novelty tolerance, outcome metric, budgets, baselines and
failure criteria on development data. Record a source hash and full plan before
using test seeds. Reserve a second untouched confirmation lot. Any retuning after
looking at failures starts a new named experiment with fresh evaluation seeds.
Report negative results and every resource component.

## Preserve physical and causal validity

Carry live charges, modes, reserve and learned settings. Admit no future outcome
before an action. Verify auxiliary separation, thresholds, caps and reserve under
every generated schedule. Cross-check representative complete online trajectories
with an independent integrator, especially near event boundaries and timing-window
edges. Include measurement noise and delays only through explicitly specified
models; do not label nominal simulations a silicon validation.

## A defensible claim

A positive result could support: "An explicitly specified R-based architecture
learns to synthesize new pulse-timing lessons that improve retained learning on
held-out pupils under the declared budgets."

It would not by itself establish semantic teaching, general pedagogy, spontaneous
invention by an isolated cell, novelty relative to all existing research, or a
hardware energy/area advantage.
