Kernel-R / Proof 12 / Composition

Every finite chain, from one local contract.

The three-cell cadence result extends to a directed chain of arbitrary finite size. The physical boxes remain at ±0.3% and ±10%, with 15 × N independent parameters. Training length and the path jitter budget are explicit assumptions.

The contract that closes

P(m, L, J) → P(m + 2, L − 4, J)

A prepared relay adds two outputs to the slow prefix, consumes four entries of the regular tail, and preserves its phase-error envelope. Each wire adds its own jitter allowance.

One cell learns two full recovery MARK doses and one ERROR gain dose. The raw-current tail and the actual changing recovery rate are included. No live state is reset.

What arbitrary depth costs

Send K ≥ 4N + 12 period-4 pulses only to the leaf after preparation. The root emits K − 2N − 3 responses and has at least eight regular-tail outputs. Every cell gains exactly 0.2 in b, and every disconnected frozen probe finishes at 8/8.

The sum of per-link jitter allowances must be at most 0.15. With fixed wire delays there is no jitter sum to accumulate, so every finite depth is covered with each fixed delay free in [0.3,0.4]. At arbitrary depth, the old ±0.05 allowance per link is not kept unchanged.

Source length and terminal time are linear in N. Total fast-phase traffic is N(K − N − 4), which is quadratic at minimum K. This is a sufficient construction, not an optimized hardware resource bound.

Proof and independent checks

The local certificate has 81 strict inequalities and 176 enclosures per physical box. A separate integer checker replays them and authenticates the preceding preparation theorem. Twelve polynomial identities check the count and resource algebra. The written packet induction establishes arbitrary finite N; a collection of finite simulations does not replace that argument.

The 21 tests cover both boxes, huge exact instances without enumerating cells, budget rejection, symbolic steps, changed assumptions, missing guards and corrupted certificates. Numerical chains of 1,3,4,16,32 cells independently reproduce the predicted counts and retained probes.

Boundary of the theorem

This is a computer-assisted written proof, not a full Lean formalization or independent mathematical peer review. It assumes a directed chain, canonical pulses, an exact source cadence, fixed device parameters and the stated preparation and jitter budgets. Arbitrary graphs, cycles, repeated lessons after saturation, general learned pedagogy and chip energy/area benefits are outside the result.
