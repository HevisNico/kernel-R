What the control shows

Reversed feedback performs equally well on these outcomes. Feedback drives the teacher's recovery b to its cap of 1.1, enabling a faster lesson. A learned choice of teaching method based on an individual student's progress has not yet been demonstrated.

18 training runs, 5,472 evaluations and 37 passing checks.

Protocol, secondary results and limitations

Exploratory numerical prototype. No reward rule or copy-selection policy was added to the R kernel.
