ROUND (the rule; no fixed time)
  ROUND is the first quicknet round whose time, 1692803367 + (ROUND - 1) * 3, is at least 600 s after t_push.
  t_push is GitHub's recorded push time of the branch_creation of the announce branch, whose only commit adds
  DRAW_ANNOUNCE.txt. So ROUND = ceil((t_push + 600 - 1692803367) / 3) + 1 (beacon_verify.round_after), t_push in
  seconds UTC.
  - The coordinator draws, and verifies the round with the frozen beacon_verify.py against the pinned key above: the
    BLS signature, and randomness = sha256(signature).
  - ONE canonical value: the beacon is drand's randomness written as 64 lowercase hex characters, exactly
    sha256(signature) in that form, with the signature exactly 48 bytes (a zero-padded signature still verifies and
    is refused) and the rounds integers; draw_seeds.draw refuses any other writing of it (upper or mixed case, spaces, a
    0x prefix) and draws the groups in its pinned GROUP_ORDER (NOT1, ANDOR3, XOR3) whatever the order of the sizes;
    analysis.py derives the value from the signature and refuses a DRAW.json that writes it otherwise. The plans
    split the groups in the same order (plan_split.ordered_groups): plan_split3.py on the DRAW.json file and the
    analysis on the draw it recomputes give byte-identical plans.
  - It pushes DRAW.json (above), so that anyone can recheck the draw offline (analysis.py test mode, announce= and
    activity=).
  - FALLBACK: if both api.drand.sh and api2.drand.sh are down at draw time, the owner fetches the round from any drand
    relay, and the frozen beacon_verify.py verifies it against the pinned key. The source of the round does not matter.
